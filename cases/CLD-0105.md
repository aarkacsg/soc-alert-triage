# CLD-0105 — Reported Payroll Email

## Alert
A user reported a payroll-themed email as suspected phishing.

## Hypotheses
**Malicious:** The email may be a credential-phishing message impersonating the organisation’s payroll system.

**Benign:** The message may be a legitimate monthly payroll notification.

## Evidence Reviewed
- Original reported email
- Sender address and sending domain
- SPF, DKIM and DMARC results
- Received-header chain
- Destination URL contained in the email
- Message content and whether credentials or payment were requested

## Verdict
**False Positive**

## Severity
**Informational**

The message was reported correctly as suspicious, but the investigation showed that it was a legitimate internal payroll notification.

## Action
- Close the incident
- Confirm to the reporter that the email is legitimate
- Thank the user for reporting the message
- Continue encouraging users to report suspicious emails

## Justification
The email authentication results aligned with the organisation’s legitimate domain, the message originated from expected infrastructure, and the link directed to the genuine HR portal without requesting credentials or payment.

## MITRE ATT&CK
Not applicable because the email was confirmed as legitimate.

## Key Learning
A payroll-themed message can closely resemble phishing, so sender authentication, header analysis, link validation and message context should all be checked before making a verdict.
